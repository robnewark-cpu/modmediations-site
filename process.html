Our Process

A Secure, Efficient Resolution Timeline

A secure, online alternative dispute process built to avoid multi-year court queues.

01

Case Submission

Parties file foundational case documents securely through our intake portal.

02

Panel Assignment

A neutral with relevant domain experience reviews the case and is assigned.

03

Resolution Session

Parties participate in private virtual or in-person sessions to reach a binding resolution.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the mediation process take?

Timelines vary by case complexity, but the process is designed to move faster than a multi-year court docket, typically moving from intake to resolution session within weeks.

Are mediation sessions conducted virtually or in person?

Both. Parties can participate in private virtual sessions or in-person sessions depending on preference and case needs.
