Frequently Asked Questions

What is MOD Mediations?

MOD Mediations (Mediators on Demand, LLC) provides neutral mediation and arbitration services for commercial, corporate, tort, and bankruptcy disputes across Texas and Oklahoma.

Is MOD Mediations affiliated with a law firm?

MOD Mediations is a separate entity from Newark Law Offices, though both are directed by the same principal, Robert C. Newark, III, Esq.

How do I submit a case for mediation?

Cases can be submitted through our online case intake portal, where you'll provide basic filer, contact, and dispute details to begin the process.

Can I apply to join the mediator panel?

Yes. Qualified arbitrators and mediators can apply to join our panel network through our panel application page.
